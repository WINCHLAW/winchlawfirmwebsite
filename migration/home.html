Welcome to Winch Law Firm, LLC.

 

 

Winch Law Firm, LLC represents landowners and businesses in matters involving pipeline servitudes, expropriation, and property rights disputes.

 

The firm’s work focuses on ensuring that acquisition efforts proceed through lawful process, transparent valuation, and full respect for private property.

Our Services

Through negotiation or litigation - and following industry-standard valuation methodologies, we achieve outcomes in which acquiring entities’ initial valuation positions are revised upward—in some instances by many multiples of the original offer—after correction of faulty framing and incomplete assumptions.
