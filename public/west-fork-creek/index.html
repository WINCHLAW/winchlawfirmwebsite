What the records show

One project, several names.

Public reporting and landowner route materials refer to the corridor as the West Fork Creek–Boeuf 500-kV Transmission Project or Project Omega. The Louisiana Public Service Commission filings use the related description West Fork Creek–St. Landry 500 kV within the broader Evest/Meta infrastructure case, LPSC Docket U-37882.

Because route descriptions and engineering plans can change, a parcel should be evaluated against the current route materials—not against a general project summary or a verbal description alone.

If surveyors, acquisition agents, or Entergy representatives have contacted you, preserve every letter, map, survey notice, offer, appraisal, and right-of-way document.
Ask the firm to review the materials.
